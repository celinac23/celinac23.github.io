I'm Celina. I study how to make machine learning models private, secure and robust, and how those guarantees trade off with what the models can do.

M.A.Sc. student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Toronto, advised by Prof. Nicolas Papernot. Researcher at the Vector Institute and the CleverHans Lab.

Focus

Two questions I keep returning to

i.

Agent security

As AI agents take on more of our work, how do we make sure every action they take keeps our systems safe?

ii.

Interpretability

How do models understand what they learn? Their internal representations may hold the key to core problems like adversarial robustness and continual learning.

Looking ahead

I believe continual learning is the most important open problem in AI: understanding how models keep learning after deployment, and catching it when that learning goes wrong. My work is a step toward it.
